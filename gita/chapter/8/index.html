Bhagavad Gita / Chapter 8

अक्षरब्रह्मयोग

Akṣara Brahma Yoga — The Yoga of the Indestructible Supreme Eternal

Arjuna opens by asking Krishna to define six technical terms — brahman, adhyatma, karma, adhibhuta, adhidaiva, adhiyajna — and what determines a person's destiny at death. Krishna answers each in turn, then centers the discourse on a single doctrine: whatever one remembers at the final moment of death is what one attains, making constant remembrance and yogic practice throughout life the real discipline. The chapter builds outward from meditation technique to cosmology — the vast day/night cycle of Brahma's world, a higher imperishable (akshara) beyond even that realm, and the two paths of light and darkness that determine return to rebirth or freedom from it — closing with a call to remain steady in yoga at all times.

What is that Eternal, what Self-knowledge, what Action, O Purushottama? And what is declared to be the knowledge of the Elements, what is called the knowledge of the Shining Ones?
